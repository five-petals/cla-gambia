Because Every Life Matters

CLA works to improve first aid knowledge and community response. Its programme focuses on preventing avoidable harm and responding safely to emergencies.

Preventable Deaths
Road, sea, workplace and home emergencies can become more severe when people lack first aid skills. CLA trains communities to respond.
Wrongful Death Announcements
An untrained person may mistake unconsciousness for death. CLA promotes community health training so people can recognize emergencies and seek appropriate care.
Our Mission
We train, educate and equip communities across The Gambia to respond safely to emergencies and help protect lives.

Two Decades of
Caring for Life

Caring for Life Association (CLA) is a Gambian-led voluntary relief charity registered in The Gambia in 2004. It provides Health & Safety training and services.

CLA's tutors, nurses, lifeguards and defensive driving instructors hold qualifications from institutions in The Gambia and abroad. The organisation has delivered training and services to institutions and individuals.

2004
CLA registered as a voluntary charity in The Gambia. Founded in Bundung as the nation's leading first aid organization.
2005
Joined the ActionAid Global campaign to fight HIV/AIDS. Began mass awareness campaigns in partnership with CSOs and community groups.
2007
Established the CLA Research Unit to support medical research, training and healthcare in The Gambia.
2011
The 2011 Health & Safety Report cited 176 work-related claims in The Gambia, including 75 workplace accidents and 51 illness claims.
Today
Continuing to serve The Gambia with First Aid training, ambulance services, HIV prevention, disaster response, and youth programmes.

Our Services & Programmes

From workplace first aid to HIV prevention, sea safety and student sponsorships, CLA serves communities across The Gambia.

First Aid Training & Services

Workplace, community and school courses cover essential, basic and activity first aid for adults, children and young people.

HIV/AIDS & Malaria Prevention

Mass awareness campaigns, peer health education, Stepping Stones programme, radio/TV spots on stigma and discrimination.

Health & Safety Audits

Proactive evaluation of safety programmes for institutions and households. Audits identify strengths, weaknesses and corrective actions.

H&S Policy Preparation

Helping organisations develop robust, compliant Health & Safety policies tailored to their specific needs.

Incident & Accident Investigation

Professional investigation of workplace incidents to identify causes and prevent recurrence.

Blood Donor Campaign

Active recruitment and awareness campaigns to grow The Gambia's voluntary blood donor base.

Integrated Health Services

Coordinated health support for communities and individuals.

Disaster Preparedness & Response

Risk reduction training and emergency response. Unplanned urbanization and extreme weather make this more critical than ever.

Home Base Care

Relief and maintenance services for the elderly, infirm, mentally and physically disabled, and other vulnerable community members.

Student Education Sponsorship

Financial sponsorship helps children access quality education.

Cleaning Campaigns

Regular clean-ups during the rainy season include cutting grass and treating stagnant water to help prevent disease.

Road Safety

Annual participation in Global Road Safety Week (April 23–29), raising awareness about road traffic injuries among young Gambians.

Sea Safety & Lifeguarding

Teaching swimming and lifeguarding to protect against drowning at Gambian beaches. Seeking life jackets, life buoys and equipment donations.

Tree Planting

CLA works with the Department of Forestry on tree planting in urban and rural areas, in support of the National Forestry Policy.

01

Research & Development

The CLA Research Unit (est. 2007) conducts medical and field research to improve health outcomes in low-income countries. Elective placements available for students (minimum 6 weeks).

02

Exchange Programmes

International exchange programmes connecting Gambian health workers and students with global counterparts to share knowledge and best practice.

03

Exchange of Information & Gifts

Global knowledge sharing, resources and in-kind gifts to support CLA's humanitarian mission across The Gambia.

HIV/AIDS Programme

Fighting the Pandemic Together

Since 2005, CLA has partnered with ActionAid, NGOs, women's groups, and communities across The Gambia to combat HIV/AIDS through education, advocacy, and solidarity.

Programme achievements

High-level awareness about HIV/AIDS raised across The Gambia through mass sensitisation campaigns.

Supported the development of the first-ever five-year HIV/AIDS strategy for NAYCO (National Association of Youths and Children's Organization).

Assisted Children Against HIV/AIDS (CAA) establish a peer learning and sharing centre.

10 illiterate women trained in adult literacy to improve their understanding of treatment literacy.

Two PLHAs sponsored for a two-week advocacy training study tour in Uganda.

Created a national network of Women Living with HIV/AIDS linked to sub-regional bodies.

Event First Aid Cover

We Cover Your Events

CLA volunteers provide first aid and medical cover at football matches, conferences, concerts and other events across The Gambia.

When large crowds gather, first aid cover helps protect attendees. CLA reports that its volunteers cover hundreds of events each year, supporting attendees until emergency services arrive. Cover is tailored to each event.

Resources We Provide:

Emergency ambulances and crew
Qualified first aiders, nurses, paramedics and doctors
Mobile treatment centres and command facilities
Cycle response teams

Events We Cover:

Football, Athletics, Swimming
Concerts and Conferences
Marathons and Road Races
Boxing and Karate
